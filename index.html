How the workflow handles key decisions

Nobody is rejected through staff review. Every family who books a slot gets the appointment. The only decision your staff makes is which confirmation email goes out — full confirmation, or confirmation plus a list of documents to bring on the day. The slot is locked either way.

The only place a submission is returned automatically is at step 5, and only for hard validation reasons: missing mandatory fields, a non-Ottawa postal code, or a clear duplicate (last name + date of birth + address all match an existing family).

Staff make a soft routing decision between two emails; the slot is locked in either case.

Operational details

  • 30-minute appointment slots, up to 10 families per slot. Seasonal calendar opens mid-August.
  • Suggested size by child's age, with last year's size plus one for returning families.
  • Point-of-sale model at the depot — server adjusts the size taken before the sale finalizes; inventory only drops when the sale finalizes.
  • $10 service fee per snowsuit, waivable per family. Daily reporting tracks paid vs waived.
  • Family ID and order numbers preserved so returning families can be looked up the same way as today.
  • Annual campaign cycle April 1 – March 31, with date filters available for ad-hoc reporting.
  • Volunteers handle pickups at the counter and only see family name and order details — no database access.
  • Existing inventory item codes preserved (e.g. S002F, S002M); jackets restructured to be split by gender.
  • Parent 1 / Parent 2 naming on the form — gender-neutral; Parent 2 mandatory only when there are two parents.
  • Newcomer eligibility category — refugee form, work permit, or PR confirmation (code 303).
  • Postal code hard-restricted to Ottawa K-prefix at the validation step.
  • Personal ID required at depot pickup for identity verification.
  • Two confirmation email types: full confirmation, or confirmation plus a list of accepted documents and how to obtain them.
  • Partial pickups handled within the same order — items marked picked vs outstanding; no new order numbers, no suffix codes. The follow-up printout shows only outstanding items so volunteers don't double-distribute.
  • Exchanges edit the existing line item; inventory adjusts automatically (returned size back into stock, new size out).
  • Inactive flag instead of deletion for old family records, with auto-flagging after a defined idle period (threshold to be confirmed).
  • Returning-family flag on the form ("Have you been here before?" plus "How did you hear about us?") for board reporting.

Open questions for your team

These are the items still requiring your team's direction before we begin building. Please discuss internally and bring the answers when we reconnect for the build walkthrough.

  1. Daily dashboard. When staff open Mareto first thing in the morning, what do they most need to see? Today's approved appointments, pending reviews, low-stock alerts, yesterday's distribution — which three would be most useful at a glance?
  2. Arabic on the public form. Bilingual English / French is confirmed. Arabic is common at the depot. Add Arabic at launch, or leave it for a later phase once the core is stable?
  3. Inactive threshold. After how many years of no activity should a family record be auto-flagged inactive? Five, seven, ten, fifteen — please confirm a number.
  4. Returning families with updated info. When a family comes back with a new address or phone, do you want a flag on the main form ("have you been here before — has anything changed?"), or a separate "update your information" path?
  5. Slot capacity changes. Day-to-day adjustments (e.g. five at lunch, ten the rest of the day) require our involvement to reconfigure. Do you anticipate this regularly, or is it rare? Routine blocking of full days is in your hands either way.
  6. Confirmation email copy. Please share your current "thanks for booking" email template so we can mirror the language exactly in the two new email types (full confirmation and confirmation-with-clarification).
  7. Size chart for first-time families. The age-to-size mapping (newborn → 6 months, etc.) — please send it once available. We'll use it as the auto-suggestion logic for new families.
